Privacy Policy for AtCoder Pulse

Effective Date: November 2026

AtCoder Pulse is committed to user privacy and open competitive programming tooling. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize any of your personal data.

1. Data Collection & Usage

AtCoder Pulse runs entirely locally on your device. The extension only accesses public competitive programming data:

  • Public AtCoder & Kenkoooo Submissions: Publicly accessible submission logs and problem difficulty estimates are fetched via open endpoints to compute your solve statistics and track unsolved problems.
  • Local Browser Storage (IndexedDB / chrome.storage): Submissions and difficulty models are cached solely within your browser storage to optimize page load speeds and prevent unnecessary network calls.

2. External Services & Third Parties

The extension communicates solely with:

  • atcoder.jp (AtCoder official contest platform)
  • kenkoooo.com (Kenkoooo AtCoder Problems API for problem models)

No user data is ever transmitted to third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising servers.

3. Changes

Any updates to this policy will be reflected in this document.

4. Contact

If you have any questions or suggestions, please visit the open-source repository.
